Privacy Policy & Security Architecture

        
      

      

        
1. Zero-Knowledge Privacy: ChronoCrypt operates on a strict zero-knowledge architecture. Your plaintext message is never transmitted to or stored on our servers. All encryption and decryption happen locally inside your web browser.

        
2. Triple-Layer Cryptographic Protection:
          
• Client-Side AES-256-GCM: Messages are encrypted locally in your browser using 256-bit authenticated encryption with the unlock timestamp bound as Additional Authenticated Data (AAD).
          
• 2-of-2 Split-Key Secret Sharing: The 256-bit key is split via XOR into two independent shares. Your Client Share remains strictly inside your link/code and is never sent to our servers.
          
• Mathematical Time-Lock (League of Entropy / drand): The Server Share is encapsulated using Identity-Based Encryption (BLS12-381 `tlock`) tied to a future cryptographic beacon round, and stored in an isolated database vault.
        

        
3. What Information We Store: We only store unreadable ciphertext, the initialization vector (IV), the target unlock timestamp, and the time-locked server capsule. We do not require accounts, emails, or personal information.

        
4. One-Time Read (Self-Destruct): If you enable "Delete Forever After First View", the server permanently deletes the key share and overwrites the ciphertext the exact moment the vault is unlocked for the first time.
